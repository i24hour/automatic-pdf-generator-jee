Q2. JEE Main 2026 (21 January Shift 1)
In an experiment the values of two spring constants were measured as k 1 = (10±0.2)N/m and
k 2 = (20±0.3)N/m. If these springs are connected in parallel, then the percentage error in equivalent spring
constant is :
(1) 1.33% (2) 1.67% (3) 2.67% (4) 2.33%

4 4

2. (2) For parallel combination: k eq = k 1 +k 2 = 10+20 = 30 N/m
For sum, absolute errors add:
Δk eq = Δk 1 +Δk 2 = 0.2+0.3 = 0.5 N/m
Percentage error = Δk eq ×100 = 0.5 ×100 = 1.67%
