7. (1) Statement I is false: Fluid pressure exists everywhere in a still fluid, not only on solid surfaces. According to
Pascal's principle, pressure at any point in a static fluid acts equally in all directions and throughout the fluid
volume.
Statement II is true: Surface tension results from excess potential energy of molecules at the liquid surface.
Surface molecules experience net inward attractive forces from interior molecules, creating an imbalance that
increases their potential energy and manifests as surface tension acting along the surface.
